Party game · 2 to 10 players

Heads
or Tell

A secret question about the people in the room. You answer with a name. Then the coin decides if everyone finds out what was asked.

  1. On your turn you get a question only you can see.
  2. Pick the person it fits best. Everyone sees the name you picked.
  3. Call the coin. Guess right and the question stays secret.
  4. Guess wrong and the question is shown to the whole room.
